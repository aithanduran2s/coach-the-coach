Coach the Coach — Setup

One time, about 5 minutes, on your phone. After this, every session is just: type two names, hit record.

Step 1 — Put the app on your phone

Open the app
  1. iPhone: tap the Share button (the square with the arrow pointing up), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  2. Android: tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen, then Install.
  3. Close the browser. From now on, open it from your home screen.

Step 2 — Get your key

The app uses Google's AI to write out the session and score it. That needs a key of your own. It's free.

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey
  2. Sign in with your Google account.
  3. Tap Create API key — top right of the screen.
  1. A box opens called Create a new key. It fills in a name for you — don't change anything.
  2. Tap Create key.
  1. A long code starting with AIza… appears. Tap the copy icon next to it.

Copy the code before you close that box. Once it's gone you'd have to make a new key.

Step 3 — Put the key in the app

  1. Open the app. Tap Setup at the top right.
  2. Paste the code into Gemini API key.
  3. Tap Test key. Wait for it to say it worked.
  4. Type your work email into Your email.
  5. Pick your Coaching feedback language — English or Spanish, whichever you'd rather read.
  6. Tap Save setup.

That's it — you're done setting up.

Every session, from now on

  1. Open the app on the Record tab.
  2. Type your name and the agent's name.
  3. Set Next 1:1 with this agent — the day and time you'll meet them again.
  4. Tap Start recording. Put the phone down. Run your session like normal.
  5. Tap Stop when you're finished.
  6. Tap Transcribe & coach and give it a couple of minutes.

You'll get back a written breakdown of the session and one thing to do differently next time.

Worth knowing

The recording stays on your phone. Nothing leaves the device until you tap "Transcribe & coach".

You'll get a calendar invite for the next 1:1 you booked. Accept it. Your phone reminds you 5 minutes before, and tapping the invite opens the app with the agent's name already filled in — so you just hit record.

Don't close the session until you've seen the feedback. The recording only lives on your phone until you transcribe it.

If something looks broken, the app says so on screen in red. Send that screenshot — don't guess.
